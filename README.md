# Project Timeline — Partners Relief & Development

A visual Gantt chart documenting the Spring 2026 capstone project for **Partners Relief & Development**, completed by Section 02 Team 02 as part of International Marketing at Purdue University.

🔗 **Live chart:** [View the timeline](https://alexagonzalestuesta.github.io/partners-gantt-s2t2/)

## About the project

Our team was tasked with designing a 5-item sustainable merchandise capsule for Partners' Kin ambassador program, a student advocacy initiative. After meeting with Partners' Strategic Engagement Manager, we expanded the scope to serve all four of the organization's donor communities (Kin, Neighbors, First Responders, and the Rose Collective) and delivered:

- A retail-grade merchandise capsule with sourced vendors and pricing
- A full strategic marketing plan
- Brand positioning and messaging frameworks
- An elevator pitch and final project report

## What this chart shows

The Gantt chart maps the full 9-week project across five parallel workstreams: the strategic marketing plan, client engagement, merchandise capsule development, the elevator pitch, and the project report. Diamond markers indicate key milestones such as the client call with Partners and the elevator pitch recording.
